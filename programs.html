Our Intervention Initiatives

Each program is designed with community needs at the center, combining medical expertise with grassroots engagement.

CardioSafe Program

Cardiovascular Health Intervention

A comprehensive community-based program targeting hypertension, heart disease risk reduction, and lifestyle modification. Includes free BP monitoring camps, dietary counseling, and exercise workshops.

Key Activities: Blood pressure screening, cholesterol testing, cooking workshops, yoga sessions, support groups for hypertensive patients.

Impact: 8,000+ people screened, 1,200 referred to treatment, 60% improvement in medication adherence among participants.

Free Screening Lifestyle Counseling Support Groups Yoga & Exercise

MindWell Initiative

Mental Health Awareness & Support

Addressing the mental health crisis through community education, stigma reduction campaigns, and accessible counseling services. We train community health workers to identify early warning signs.

Key Activities: School-based mental health workshops, teacher training, free counseling camps, helpline service, peer support groups.

Impact: 200+ schools covered, 5,000+ students educated, 800+ free counseling sessions provided.

School Programs Free Counseling Stigma Reduction Helpline

DiabetesAware Program

Diabetes Prevention & Management

Targeting the diabetes epidemic through early screening, dietary education, and long-term disease management support. Special focus on rural communities where access to care is limited.

Key Activities: Blood glucose screening, HbA1c testing, diabetic diet workshops, foot care education, insulin management guidance.

Impact: 12,000+ screened, 2,500 newly diagnosed connected to care, 40% reduction in hospitalizations among enrolled patients.

Blood Sugar Screening Diet Education Rural Outreach Disease Management

HealthyMom & Child

Maternal & Child Health Program

Comprehensive maternal and child health program ensuring every mother and child has access to essential health services, nutrition counseling, and immunization support.

Key Activities: Antenatal care, nutritional supplementation, immunization drives, safe delivery workshops, postpartum mental health support.

Impact: 3,000+ mothers enrolled, 95% immunization coverage in target villages, 30% reduction in low birth weight infants.

Antenatal Care Immunization Nutrition Postpartum Support

BreatheFree Campaign

Respiratory Health & Anti-Tobacco

Fighting respiratory disease through anti-tobacco campaigns, air quality awareness, and treatment support for chronic respiratory conditions like asthma and COPD.

Key Activities: Spirometry testing, smoking cessation clinics, air quality monitoring education, inhalation device training, school anti-tobacco programs.

Impact: 1,500+ people helped quit smoking, 4,000 COPD/asthma patients enrolled, 60+ schools declared tobacco-free.

Anti-Tobacco Smoking Cessation Asthma Care Air Quality

InfectShield Program

Infectious Disease Control

Comprehensive infectious disease control targeting HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and vector-borne diseases through awareness, testing, treatment linkage, and hygiene promotion.

Key Activities: HIV testing camps, TB DOTS support, dengue/malaria prevention drives, hand hygiene campaigns, condom distribution, water purification education.

Impact: 6,000+ HIV tests conducted, 400 TB patients on DOTS, 70% reduction in dengue cases in target areas.

HIV/AIDS Tuberculosis Vaccination Hygiene Promotion
Our Approach

How Our Programs Work

A structured, community-centered process ensures maximum reach and sustainable impact.

1

Community Assessment

Identify priority health needs through surveys, data analysis, and community consultations.

2

Program Design

Develop culturally appropriate, evidence-based interventions with medical expert input.

3

Implementation

Deploy trained health workers and volunteers to deliver programs in schools, villages, and workplaces.

4

Monitoring & Evaluation

Track outcomes, measure impact, and continuously improve program effectiveness.
