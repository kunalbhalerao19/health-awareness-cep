Health Tips

Essential Health Awareness Tips

🥗 Nutrition & Diet

  • Eat at least 5 servings of fruits and vegetables daily
  • Reduce salt intake to less than 5g per day to prevent hypertension
  • Avoid trans fats and limit saturated fats
  • Choose whole grains over refined carbohydrates
  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
  • Limit sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed foods

🏃 Physical Activity

  • At least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week
  • Include strength training exercises 2 days a week
  • Reduce sedentary time — take breaks every 30 minutes
  • Walk 10,000 steps a day for cardiovascular benefits
  • Practice yoga or stretching for flexibility and stress relief
  • Children should have 60+ minutes of physical activity daily

🧘 Mental Wellness

  • Practice mindfulness or meditation for 10 minutes daily
  • Maintain social connections — isolation harms mental health
  • Seek professional help without shame — mental illness is treatable
  • Get 7–9 hours of quality sleep every night
  • Limit social media use to reduce anxiety and comparison
  • Engage in hobbies and activities that bring joy

🩺 Preventive Health

  • Get regular health check-ups even when feeling well
  • Stay up to date on all recommended vaccinations
  • Know your numbers: BP, blood sugar, cholesterol, BMI
  • Do not self-medicate — always consult a qualified doctor
  • Quit tobacco in any form — it is the #1 preventable cause of death
  • Practice safe sex and get regular STI screenings
