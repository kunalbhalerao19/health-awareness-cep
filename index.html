About Us

What We Do

HealthFirst is a community-driven initiative under the Community Engagement Project (CEP) dedicated to addressing public health challenges through evidence-based intervention and awareness campaigns.

We partner with local governments, NGOs, schools, and healthcare providers to design programs that are culturally sensitive, accessible, and impactful.

  • Evidence-based health interventions
  • Community-centered awareness campaigns
  • Free health screening & counseling
  • School & workplace wellness programs

Focus Areas

Our Health Priorities

We target the most critical health challenges facing our communities today.

Cardiovascular Health

Awareness programs on hypertension, heart disease risk factors, and lifestyle modifications to prevent cardiovascular events.

Learn more

Mental Health

Destigmatizing mental illness, providing counseling resources, and training community health workers to identify early signs.

Learn more

Diabetes Management

Screening programs, dietary counseling, and support groups for type 2 diabetes prevention and management.

Learn more

Maternal & Child Health

Prenatal care guidance, immunization awareness, nutrition programs for mothers and children under five.

Learn more

Respiratory Health

Anti-tobacco campaigns, air quality awareness, and management programs for asthma and COPD patients.

Learn more

Infectious Disease

HIV/AIDS awareness, tuberculosis control, vaccination drives, and hygiene promotion in high-risk communities.
